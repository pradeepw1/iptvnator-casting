The desktop player can now cast to a Chromecast or Roku on your network: pick
your TV from the new Cast button and control playback from the player while you
watch. HLS and MP4 streams work best; raw MPEG-TS streams and streams that need
custom headers may not play. Roku TVs need "Control by mobile apps" set to
Default.
